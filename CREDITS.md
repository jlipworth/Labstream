## Changes

The posters and backdrops shown in the app are crops and resizes of single frames from each film.
In screenshots they appear inside Labstream's own interface, and the playback screenshots show a
frame of the film with the player controls over it. The films themselves are served complete and
unmodified, including their end credits. The exact sources, crops and checksums are recorded in
the
[review-media manifest](https://github.com/jlipworth/Labstream/blob/main/scripts/app-review-media-provenance.json).
